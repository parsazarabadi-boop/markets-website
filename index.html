FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Zapline custodial?
No. Zapline never holds your keys or your funds. The app constructs transactions and your wallet signs them locally.
Which chains are supported?
The current release supports three EVM networks with a shared state layer. Additional chains roll out to Pro users first.
How is execution time measured?
From the moment you confirm to the moment the transaction is included in a block. 0.42s is the rolling 30-day median.
Can I revoke access for a connected app?
Yes. Open Settings → Connected apps and revoke any app instantly. Revocation takes effect on the next signature request.
What do I get with Zapline Pro?
Priority routing, unlimited snipes, twelve months of history export, and early access to new chains.
How are referral rewards paid?
Rewards accrue continuously and settle to your wallet every Friday. You can claim early for a small network fee.
